Research on misleading visualizations connects two
lines.
The first studies how charts mislead human readers:
deception relies less on outright distortion than on
faithful drawings with selective context
\citep{lisnic2023misleading, nadib2026guardrail}.
The second measures the phenomenon in models:
benchmarks evaluate multimodal LLMs on misleading chart
QA \citep{chen2025unmasking, bharti2024chartom,
alexander2024models}, bias across interpretations is
documented \citep{rahman2026same}, and agent
recognition of misleadingness is probed
\citep{panda2026making, blasilli2026true,
seto2026llms}; manipulations are
quantified with mitigations \citep{mahbub2025perils,
mahbub2026chart}, and method-side responses include
tool-augmented reasoning \citep{kaur2025chartagent},
structured extraction \citep{das2025chartsofthought},
detection \citep{tonglet2025this}, and dual-path
frameworks \citep{zhang2026navigating}.
The closest model-level defense, tabular answering and
re-plotting \citep{tonglet2025protecting}, repairs a
single question--answer round, while \ours{} targets
the interpretation rules that recur across an executing
agent's rounds and maintains their validated revisions
in runtime state.

A second line concerns agent-environment integrity:
injection benchmarks show that tool and web content can
steer agents \citep{zhan2024injecagent}, security
benchmarks measure targeted hijacking
\citep{evtimov2025wasp}, injection reaches perceived
representations such as HTML accessibility trees
\citep{johnson2025manipulating}, red-teaming and
runtime frameworks generate and intercept attacks at
scale \citep{wang2025agentvigil, zhao2026clawguard},
and interface-level defenses separate instructions from
data \citep{chen2024struq} or train against injected
content \citep{chen2024secalign}; reflection and tree
search open new vulnerabilities when the evaluator is
exposed to attacked content \citep{wu2024dissecting}.
\ours{} protects a different layer: not the instruction
hierarchy but the interpretation layer between visual
observation and task judgment, where a misleading chart
exerts its effect without any injected instruction.

The third line concerns persistent state and
correction: verbal reinforcement carries feedback
across attempts \citep{shinn2023reflexion},
self-consistency aggregates reasoning paths
\citep{wang2022selfconsistency}, intrinsic
self-correction without external feedback degrades
reasoning \citep{huang2023large, li2025decomposing},
surveys organize the state and memory design space
\citep{wang2024survey, hu2025agents,
zhou2026ready},
and realistic
visual web tasks remain hard enough that
single-decision correctness cannot be taken for granted
\citep{koh2024visualwebarena}.
\ours{} borrows the state-carrying idea but changes the
carried content: the state records interpretation rules
with support, scope, and revision history, so later steps inherit what was verified about the
chart, not a verdict about the answer.
